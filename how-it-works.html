ABOUT THINKRUSH

Quick games for focused thinking.

ThinkRush is a browser-first collection of small mental challenges. The idea is simple: choose a game, play a focused round, learn from the result, and try again.

What is included?

Quiz, Sudoku, Tic-Tac-Toe, Chess, Memory Match, Pattern Recognition, Number Challenge, Reaction Test, Word Puzzle, Logic Puzzle, and 2048.

Why no account?

Games are designed to work without registration. This keeps the experience fast and avoids asking people for unnecessary personal information.

What happens to scores?

Where useful, scores such as a 2048 best score are saved locally in your own browser. ThinkRush does not need a server-side profile for normal play.

Built to grow

Each game is an independent module, so new games can be added without redesigning the whole site.
