PRIVACY

Simple by design.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

No account is required

ThinkRush is designed to let people play without creating an account or submitting personal information.

Local browser storage

Some games may save a best score or small preference locally in your browser. This information stays on your device unless you clear your browser storage.

Contact

The contact page is configured as a mailto link. A message only reaches the email provider you choose when you press send.

Analytics

This project does not include Google Analytics, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting by default.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the site's data practices change. Update the date and text before publishing any new third-party services.
